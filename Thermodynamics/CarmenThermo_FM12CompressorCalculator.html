  1. Compare the two pressure-flow curves. Explain how changing speed shifts the compressor operating curve.
  2. Identify the best measured efficiency point at each speed. Does the best operating point occur at the same restriction?
  3. At each restriction, evaluate whether flow, total pressure, and fluid power follow the affinity laws. Use calculated deviations as evidence.
  4. Which quantity shows the greatest deviation from ideal affinity-law scaling? Discuss plausible experimental and mechanical reasons.

  6. Would continuous operation at the highest-pressure or highest-flow condition be advisable? Discuss pressure, flow, power, and efficiency.
  7. Compare the experimental curve family with published centrifugal-compressor curves from a reputable engineering source.
  8. Explain how the findings would influence compressor selection or variable-speed control in industry.
