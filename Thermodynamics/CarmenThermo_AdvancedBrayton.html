Compression and expansion stage counts are independent. A single compressor uses the entire overall pressure ratio; two compressors use √rₚ per stage. A single turbine uses the entire overall pressure ratio; two turbines use √rₚ per stage.

Actual regenerator: T₅ = T₄ + ε(T₉ − T₄). For equal heat-capacity rates, T₁₀ = T₉ − (T₅ − T₄). Regeneration is used only when T₉ > T₄.

The ideal-results tab intentionally uses ε = 1.00. The actual-results tab uses the effectiveness entered above.
