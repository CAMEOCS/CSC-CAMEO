Gamma Stirling Engine: Gas Motion & Two-Plane Crank Mechanism

LEFT: Displacer, moves working gasRIGHT: Power piston, sealedCENTER: Enlarged gas path + regenerator · Rods shown in separate axial planes

Watch the enlarged gas path: ● hot gas → ● regenerator → ● cold gas. colored particles make the sealed working-gas motion easier to see. The enlarged passage and displacer clearance are intentionally schematic, not drawn to scale. The displacer moves the working gas between hot and cold regions; the separate power piston responds to gas pressure and produces useful work. The working gas remains sealed inside the engine.
