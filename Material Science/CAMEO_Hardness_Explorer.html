Important: Cross-scale conversions are approximate and material-dependent. This Explorer uses an educational steel reference dataset with linear interpolation. Use direct testing in the required scale for specifications or acceptance decisions.

Why the numbers are not exact equivalents

Brinell, Rockwell, and Vickers tests use different indenters, loads, and measurement methods. Their relationships are empirical rather than a single universal equation. A scale may show N/A when the selected hardness lies outside its useful reference range.
