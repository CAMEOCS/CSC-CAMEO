Educational model: The nonsteady solution assumes a semi-infinite solid, constant surface concentration, constant diffusivity, and a uniform initial concentration. The animation is schematic.

What to notice

Raise the temperature and watch diffusivity increase. Increase time and the concentration profile penetrates farther into the solid. The animated atoms wander randomly, while their overall distribution spreads from higher toward lower concentration.
