Welcome to CAMEO

CAMEO means Computer-Aided Modeling and Engineering Online.

Use these interactive engineering tools to explore models, compare cases, and verify engineering calculations.

How to use the portal

  1. Select Tool Catalog.
  2. Open the engineering discipline you need.
  3. Select Open Tool for the desired solver or explorer.
