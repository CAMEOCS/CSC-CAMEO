5. Research logic and limitations

Geometry + material state → M(k), K(k), C(k) → Hphysics(ω,k) → Hrelative(ω,k) → regenerative stability lobes

The physics pathway uses an Euler-Bernoulli cantilever approximation for the first bending mode and a scaled second mode. The measured pathway accepts an experimentally identified relative cutting-point FRF. The regenerative solver uses a zero-order frequency-domain construction. Research use still requires complete directional complex FRFs Hxx, Hxy, Hyx, Hyy and benchmarking against published Altintas/CutPro cases.
